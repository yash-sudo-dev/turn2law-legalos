Security and privacy

Quietly, seriously, by design.

Turn2Law processes sensitive legal and business documents. Here is the security model the platform is built on, stated as commitments, not marketing.

Tenant isolation

Every record is scoped to its organisation. Access is enforced server-side with row-level checks, never by the frontend alone.

Private document storage

Documents live in private storage and are served only through short-lived signed URLs. No public buckets.

Encryption and least privilege

Encryption in transit and at rest. Risk-based MFA, least-privilege roles, and audited break-glass for administrators.

Append-only audit logs

Every consequential action is logged to an append-only store. Access reviews are periodic and documented.

Data residency in India

Workloads and managed services run in Indian regions. A named subprocessor list is maintained and reviewed.

Your documents are not training data

Confidential documents are not used to train public models. Production data is never used in development or testing.

No certification badges appear here. Independent audits, penetration tests, and certifications will be listed in this section only when they exist and can be named.
